# Kiling.Ritual

#  How to Play

Welcome to the ultimate wave-based survival shooter! Work with your team to survive as many rounds as possible against increasingly difficult hordes of enemies. 

You need to use Radmin or Hamachi to play online with a friend. Just paste their IP address and press join once they host the game.

###  The Core Loop
*   **Survive the Wave:** Eliminate all enemies to clear the current round. Every wave, enemies spawn in greater numbers and with more health. Watch out for Boss rounds every 5 waves!
*   **Earn Points:** Damaging and killing enemies earns you cash.
*   **Visit the Trader:** Between each wave, you get a 30-second break. Find the **Trader** that spawns on the map to spend your points on better weapons, attachments, and ammo before they despawn.

###  Teamwork & Survival
You are heavily reliant on your team to make it to the higher rounds. 
*   **Going Down:** If your health hits zero, you are "Downed". You will fall to the ground and begin bleeding out. Surviving teammates can hold the interact key to revive you.
*   **Spectating:** If you bleed out completely, you die. You can use **Left/Right Click** to spectate the screens of your surviving teammates.
*   **Respawning:** Death is not the end! If your alive teammates successfully clear the current wave, all dead players will automatically respawn at the Trader with full health for the next round.
